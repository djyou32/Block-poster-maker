## Implementation checks

- README and HTML share a single resource dataset: 18 unique URLs across five categories.
- All resource descriptions and links are present in the initial static HTML. Search and category filters progressively enhance the page.
- Local asset paths, document links and fragment targets resolve; HTML IDs are unique.
- JSON-LD describes the visible collection with WebSite, CollectionPage and an 18-entry ItemList. There are no invented ratings or rich-result promises.
- Sitemap contains the canonical, indexable page with priority 1.0. The 404 page is excluded and declares noindex.
- PNG favicon is 96 × 96; ICO includes 16, 32 and 48 pixel sizes. Social image is 1200 × 630.
- Browser checks covered search, no matches, category selection, navigation resetting filters, and horizontal overflow at 1280, 390 and 310 pixel viewport widths.
- External URL requests returned 200 for 15 entries. Adobe and the National Gallery returned 403, and The Met returned 429; their relevant official content was reviewed through web retrieval. Those responses are access/rate restrictions, not evidence that the resources are dead.

These checks establish content and implementation consistency. They do not establish Google indexing, ranking, rich-result eligibility, or physical printing quality. Source review does not mean every external application was tested hands-on.
